Chapter 02 — Powertrain

Combustion Meets Current

A flat-plane V8 drives the rear wheels through a hybrid transmission with its own axial-flux motor. Two more axial-flux motors power the front axle independently — giving the Nuvolari on-demand all-wheel drive with no mechanical link to the front.

01

Engine

4.0L Twin-Turbo V8

  • Flat-plane crankshaft
  • Dry-sump lubrication
  • Mid-mounted, rear-wheel drive
02

Electric Motors

3× Axial-Flux

  • 1 integrated into the transmission — rear
  • 2 driving the front axle independently
  • Electric-only, on-demand AWD
03

Battery

7.3 kWh

  • Plug-in hybrid pack
  • Mounted low, central tunnel
  • Charges under hard braking
